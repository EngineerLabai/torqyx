<Callout type="info" title="Engineering Usage Note">
This guide details industrial surface engineering, plating thicknesses, corrosion protection ratings, and dimensional tolerances according to ISO 1461, ISO 7599, ISO 4527, and ISO 9227 salt spray tests. For high-tensile Grade 10.9 and 12.9 fasteners, hydrogen de-embrittlement baking according to ISO 4042 is strictly mandatory.
</Callout>

## Problem / Objective

Even the highest-grade engineering alloys degrade catastrophically if exposed to harsh service environments without appropriate surface protection. Misjudging surface treatment parameters introduces severe engineering liabilities:
- Coating build-up ($5 - 80\ \mu\text{m}$) violating precision fit tolerances, jamming bearing journals and seizing threaded fasteners,
- Hydrogen embrittlement cracking in high-strength fasteners induced by acid pickling and electroplating, causing delayed brittle fracture under tension,
- Accelerating localized galvanic corrosion when incompatible plating metals contact the underlying substrate.

## Assumptions

- Atmospheric corrosivity categories are defined according to ISO 12944 (C1 through C5-M).
- Coating thickness build-up is factored into pre-plate machining drawing tolerances.
- External sharp edges feature generous corner break radii ($R \ge 1.0\text{ mm}$) to ensure uniform barrier thickness.

## Step by step method

1. **Select Coating Process for Application Duty:**
   - **Hot-Dip Galvanizing (HDG - ISO 1461):** $45 - 85\ \mu\text{m}$ thickness; sacrificial cathodic protection providing 30+ years outdoor service life for structural steel.
   - **Electro-Galvanized Zinc (ISO 2081):** $8 - 15\ \mu\text{m}$ thickness with blue/yellow trivalent passivation; cost-effective corrosion protection for precision mechanical fasteners.
   - **Hard Anodizing / Type III (ISO 7599):** $25 - 50\ \mu\text{m}$ ceramic alumina layer; imparts $60\text{ HRC}$ equivalent surface hardness, dielectric isolation, and wear resistance to aluminum.
   - **Electroless Nickel (ENP - ISO 4527):** $10 - 25\ \mu\text{m}$ uniform autocatalytic deposit; 100% uniform thickness in blind bores, $50 - 65\text{ HRC}$ hardness, and superior chemical resistance.
   - **Hard Chrome Plating:** $20 - 100\ \mu\text{m}$; standard for hydraulic cylinder rods, low friction coefficient ($\mu \approx 0.12$), and $68 - 72\text{ HRC}$ abrasion resistance.

2. **Calculate Dimensional Shift from Plating Layer Build-Up:**
   External diameters increase by double the coating thickness ($t$), while internal bore diameters decrease:
   $$D_{plated} = D_{machined} + 2 \cdot t, \quad d_{bore,plated} = d_{bore,machined} - 2 \cdot t$$
   On standard $60^\circ$ metric screw threads, flank deposition shifts pitch diameter by four times coating thickness:
   $$\Delta d_{pitch} = 4 \cdot t$$
   *(e.g., A $12\ \mu\text{m}$ zinc layer expands screw pitch diameter by $48\ \mu\text{m}$, consuming the entire 6g tolerance allowance).*

3. **Mandate Hydrogen De-Embrittlement Baking for High-Strength Fasteners:**
   Per ISO 4042, steels with tensile strength $R_m \ge 1000\text{ MPa}$ (especially 10.9 and 12.9 grade bolts) must be baked at $200 - 220\text{ °C}$ for 4 to 24 hours **within 4 hours** of electroplating to drive out trapped atomic hydrogen.

4. **Specify Neutral Salt Spray (ISO 9227 NSS) Exposure Benchmarks:**
   - Commercial zinc with blue trivalent passivate: 72 - 120 hours to white rust, 240 hours to red rust.
   - Zinc-Nickel alloy plating (12-15% Ni): 720 - 1000 hours red rust resistance.
   - Electroless Nickel (25 $\mu\text{m}$ high-phosphorus): 500+ hours neutral salt spray resistance.

5. **Industrial Worked Example:**
   A precision journal shaft of $d = 25\text{ mm}$ with h6 tolerance ($0 / -9\ \mu\text{m} \implies 24.991 - 25.000\text{ mm}$) requires $15 \pm 3\ \mu\text{m}$ electroless nickel plating:
   - Bilateral coating build-up: $2 \times (12\dots 18\ \mu\text{m}) = +24\dots +36\ \mu\text{m}$.
   - If machined to nominal 25.000 mm before plating, final diameter becomes $25.024 - 25.036\text{ mm}$, completely failing the bearing fit!
   - **Correct Engineering Solution:** Pre-plate machining dimensions are derated:
     $$d_{pre-plate} = [24.991 - 0.036, \ 25.000 - 0.024] = \mathbf{24.955\dots 24.976\text{ mm}}$$
   - Post-plating dimensions will land precisely within the intended h6 tolerance limits.

## Common mistakes

- **Failing to Undersize Thread Flanks Before Plating:** Applying $20\ \mu\text{m}$ zinc to standard 6g bolts prevents nuts from threading on assembly lines.
- **Overlooking 50% Penetration in Hard Anodizing:** Type III anodizing grows 50% inward into the substrate and 50% outward beyond the original surface.
- **Omitting Vent and Drain Holes for Hot-Dip Galvanizing:** Dipping enclosed tubular weldments into $450\text{ °C}$ molten zinc without pressure relief holes causes explosive blowouts.
- **Omitting Hydrogen Bake Documentation on Critical Bolts:** Unbaked 10.9/12.9 bolts crack under preload within hours of assembly through silent delayed hydrogen fracture.
