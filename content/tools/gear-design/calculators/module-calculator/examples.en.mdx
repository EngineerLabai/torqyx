## Example 1: Compute Module from Pitch Diameter

Calculating standard module from an existing machine layout.

**Inputs**
- Pitch diameter: $40\text{ mm}$
- Number of teeth: $20$

**Outputs**
- Calculated module: $m = 2.0\text{ mm}$
- Circular pitch: $p = 6.283\text{ mm}$
- Tip diameter ($d_a$): $44.0\text{ mm}$

**Notes**
- Standard ISO 54 Series 1 module; no tooling penalty.

## Example 2: Non-Standard Pitch Standardization

Converting preliminary envelope dimensions into standardized gear parameters.

**Inputs**
- Target pitch diameter: $92\text{ mm}$
- Number of teeth: $30$

**Outputs**
- Raw module: $m = 92 / 30 = 3.067\text{ mm}$
- Recommended standard module: $m_{std} = 3.0\text{ mm}$
- Adjusted standard pitch diameter: $D_{std} = 3.0 \times 30 = 90.0\text{ mm}$
- Diameter deviation: $-2.17\%$

**Notes**
- Adjust operating center distance $a$ to accommodate standard $m = 3\text{ mm}$ rather than machining custom tooling.
