<Callout type="info" title="Engineering Scope">
This tool calculates the raw gear module from operating pitch diameter and tooth count, aligns the pitch with standard ISO 54 / DIN 780 modular series, and verifies minimum tooth thresholds against kinematic undercut risks.
</Callout>

## Governing Formulas & Methodology

Raw module (m) and standard reference pitch diameter (D_std):

<Formula label="Module and Pitch Diameter">
m_raw = D / z | D_std = m_std · z | Deviation (%) = |D_std - D| / D × 100
</Formula>

Under standard 20° normal pressure angle without positive profile modification, the theoretical threshold for undercut is governed by:

<Formula label="Undercut Limit">
z_min = 2 / sin²(α) = 2 / sin²(20°) ≈ 17.1 (z &lt; 17 requires positive addendum correction)
</Formula>

## Manufacturing & Quality Considerations

<Callout type="warning" title="Tooling & Cost Impacts">
Specifying non-standard custom gear modules necessitates dedicated custom gear hobs, shaper cutters, and grinding profiles, significantly inflating tooling lead time and manufacturing costs. Always design around standard ISO 54 Series 1 or Series 2 modules.
</Callout>
