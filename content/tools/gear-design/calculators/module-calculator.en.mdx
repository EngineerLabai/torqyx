## Calculation Explanation

<Callout type="info" title="How to use">
<StepList title="Step-by-step sizing" steps={["Enter pitch diameter (d) and tooth count (z)", "Review calculated raw module (m)", "Compare with recommended ISO 54 standard module series"]} />
</Callout>

<Formula label="Module and Pitch">
m = d / z
p = π · m
d_std = m_std · z
</Formula>

**Assumptions**
- Standard ISO 54 Preferred Series 1 (1, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16 mm).
- Zero profile shift (x = 0); geometry is referenced to the standard pitch circle.
- Standard normal pressure angle α = 20°.

**Units**
- Pitch diameter (d), standard pitch diameter (d_std): mm
- Module (m), pitch (p): mm
- Tooth count (z): dimensionless integer

**Engineering Standards**
- ISO 54:1996 (Cylindrical gears for general engineering and heavy engineering — Modules)
- ISO 53:1998 (Cylindrical gears — Basic rack profile for general engineering)
- DIN 780 Series 1 and Series 2 preferred modular pitches

<Callout type="warning" title="Design Limitations & Undercut Criteria">
For standard 20° involute gears without positive profile shift, tooth undercut occurs when z &lt; z_min = 2 / sin²(20°) ≈ 17. Sizing non-standard modules requires dedicated custom hob tooling and significantly increases machining costs.
</Callout>

## Examples

### Example 1: Standard Spur Gear Pitch and Module Check

Determine module and circular pitch for an industrial spur pinion.

**Inputs**
- Pitch diameter d = 200 mm
- Number of teeth z = 40

**Outputs**
- Raw module m = 5.000 mm
- Circular pitch p = 15.708 mm
- Selected standard module m_std = 5.0 mm (ISO 54 Series 1)
- Standard pitch circle d_std = 200.0 mm
- Addendum ha = 5.0 mm, Dedendum hf = 6.25 mm
- Tip diameter da = d + 2m = 210.0 mm

### Example 2: Non-Standard Pitch Diameter Rounding

Sizing a reduction gear with non-standard preliminary spacing.

**Inputs**
- Pitch diameter d = 125 mm
- Number of teeth z = 38

**Outputs**
- Raw module m = 125 / 38 = 3.289 mm
- Nearest ISO 54 standard module: m = 3.5 mm (Series 2) or m = 3.0 mm (Series 1)
- If m_std = 3.0 mm selected: adjusted operating pitch diameter d_std = 3.0 × 38 = 114.0 mm
- If m_std = 3.5 mm selected: adjusted operating pitch diameter d_std = 3.5 × 38 = 133.0 mm
