## Example 1: Helical Mesh Sizing

Checking contact ratio optimization for a turbine reduction gearbox.

**Inputs**
- Normal module: $m_n = 3.0\text{ mm}$
- Pinion: $z_1 = 21$, Gear: $z_2 = 63$
- Pressure angle: $\alpha_n = 20^\circ$, Helix angle: $\beta = 18^\circ$
- Face width: $b = 45\text{ mm}$

**Outputs**
- Transverse module: $m_t = 3.154\text{ mm}$
- Transverse contact ratio: $\epsilon_\alpha = 1.624$
- Overlap ratio: $\epsilon_\beta = 1.475$
- Total contact ratio: $\epsilon_\gamma = 3.099$

## Example 2: Low-Teeth Pinion Spur Check

Investigating boundary conditions on an 13-tooth pinion spur pair.

**Inputs**
- Module: $m = 5.0\text{ mm}$
- Pinion: $z_1 = 13$, Gear: $z_2 = 26$
- Pressure angle: $\alpha = 20^\circ$

**Outputs**
- Profile contact ratio: $\epsilon_\alpha = 1.285$
- Note: Contact ratio meets the bare minimum criterion ($> 1.20$), but tooth profile undercut check or positive addendum shift is strongly advised.
