<Callout type="info" title="Engineering Scope">
This tool calculates the transverse contact ratio (e_α), face overlap ratio (e_β), and total contact ratio (e_γ) for cylindrical spur and helical gear pairs in compliance with ISO 21771 and ISO 6336-1.
</Callout>

## Governing Formulas & Methodology

Transverse geometry transformations:

<Formula label="Involute Geometry Transformations">
m_t = m_n / cos(β) | tan(α_t) = tan(α_n) / cos(β) | p_b = π · m_t · cos(α_t)
</Formula>

Path of contact and transverse contact ratio (e_α):

<Formula label="Path of Contact Length">
g_α = √(ra1² - rb1²) + √(ra2² - rb2²) - a · sin(α_t) | e_α = g_α / p_b
</Formula>

Overlap and total contact ratio:

<Formula label="Overlap and Total Ratio">
e_β = (b · sin(β)) / (π · m_n) | e_γ = e_α + e_β
</Formula>

## Engineering Recommendations

<Callout type="warning" title="Design Criteria">
- If e_α &lt; 1.0, the preceding tooth pair disengages before the succeeding pair enters mesh, producing severe dynamic impact shocks.
- For industrial gearing, specify e_α ≥ 1.4 for spur and e_γ ≥ 2.0 for helical gears to ensure low acoustic noise and smooth transmission error signatures.
</Callout>
