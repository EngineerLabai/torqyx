<Callout type="info" title="Engineering Scope">
This tool calculates the axial thrust (Fa), tangential driving (Ft), and radial separating (Fr) forces produced by single helical gearing, enabling accurate sizing of thrust bearings and shaft bending fatigue checks.
</Callout>

## Governing Formulas & Methodology

Torque and tangential load:

<Formula label="Torque and Tangential Force">
T = 9550 · P / n | Ft = 2000 · T / d
</Formula>

Axial thrust and transverse radial separation:

<Formula label="Axial and Radial Force Resolution">
Fa = Ft · tan(β) | Fr = Ft · (tan(α_n) / cos(β))
</Formula>

## Hand of Helix and Thrust Direction Rules

<Callout type="warning" title="Determining Thrust Vector Direction">
- Right-Hand Helix (RH): Viewed along the shaft with clockwise rotation pushing away, thrust is directed toward the observer.
- Opposite Hands: Mating external helical gears must have opposite hands (RH pinion meshes with LH gear).
- Thrust Reversal: Under electric braking, regenerative torque, or reverse rotation, axial thrust reverses direction 180°.
</Callout>
