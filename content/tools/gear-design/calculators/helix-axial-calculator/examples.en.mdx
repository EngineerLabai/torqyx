## Example 1: Helical Pinion Thrust Reaction

Determining locating bearing thrust force for an electric drivetrain.

**Inputs**
- Power: $P = 22\text{ kW}$
- Speed: $n = 2900\text{ rpm}$
- Pitch diameter: $d = 65\text{ mm}$
- Helix angle: $\beta = 18^\circ$
- Pressure angle: $\alpha_n = 20^\circ$

**Outputs**
- Torque: $T = 72.45\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$
- Tangential force: $F_t = 2229.2\text{ N}$
- Axial thrust: $F_a = 2229.2 \times \tan(18^\circ) = 724.3\text{ N}$
- Radial force: $F_r = 2229.2 \times (\tan(20^\circ)/\cos(18^\circ)) = 853.1\text{ N}$

## Example 2: Double Helical Comparison

Comparing single helical with opposed herringbone layout.

**Inputs**
- Tangential load: $F_t = 12000\text{ N}$
- Helix angle: $\beta = 22^\circ$

**Outputs**
- Single helical axial force: $F_a = 12000 \times \tan(22^\circ) = 4848.3\text{ N}$ (must be carried by bearings).
- Double helical / herringbone: Net external $F_a = 0\text{ N}$ as opposing helices balance internal thrust on the same rotor.
