## Calculation Explanation

<Callout type="info" title="How to use">
<StepList title="Step-by-step viscosity selection" steps={["Enter specific tooth load factor (ks in N/mm) and pitch line velocity (v in m/s)", "Select lubrication method (splash, dip, or forced circulating oil) and operating sump temperature (°C)", "Read recommended ISO VG viscosity grade and operational tolerance band conforming to ISO 3448 and AGMA 9005"]} />
</Callout>

<Formula label="Lubrication Selection Index">
Index Factor = ks / v
v = (π · d · n) / 60000
ks = (Ft / b) · KA
</Formula>

**Assumptions**
- Industrial enclosed cylindrical and bevel gear drives operating under elastohydrodynamic lubrication (EHL) regime.
- Mineral or PAO synthetic gear oils containing extreme pressure (EP) anti-wear additives conforming to DIN 51517 Part 3 (CLP).
- Sump temperature normalized around standard 40°C ISO rating baseline.

**Units**
- Specific tooth load (ks): N/mm
- Pitch line velocity (v): m/s
- Operating temperature (T): °C
- Kinematic viscosity: mm²/s (cSt) at 40°C (ISO VG)

**Engineering Standards**
- ISO 3448:1992 (Industrial liquid lubricants — ISO viscosity classification)
- AGMA 9005-F16 (Industrial Gear Lubrication)
- DIN 51517-3:2018 (Lubricants — Lubricating oils — Part 3: CLP lubricating oils; Minimum requirements)

<Callout type="warning" title="Viscosity-Temperature Relationship">
Oil viscosity drops exponentially with increasing temperature. If the gearbox sump exceeds 75°C under continuous duty, select one ISO VG grade higher or switch to high-viscosity-index synthetic polyalphaolefin (PAO) base stocks to maintain minimum EHL film thickness and prevent micropitting.
</Callout>

## Examples

### Example 1: Splash-Lubricated Industrial Speed Reducer

Determining oil viscosity for an intermediate industrial conveyor reducer.

**Inputs**
- Specific load factor: ks = 25 N/mm
- Pitch line velocity: v = 12 m/s
- Lubrication method: Splash (oil bath)
- Operating temperature: T = 70 °C

**Outputs**
- Index factor: ks / v = 25 / 12 = 2.083
- Baseline viscosity at 40°C: ISO VG 100
- Temperature-adjusted operating recommendation: ISO VG 100 to ISO VG 150 (AGMA 3EP / 4EP)
- Ensures continuous splash distribution without excessive churning power loss.

### Example 2: Heavy-Duty Low-Speed Mill Drive

Selecting lubricant for high-load, low-speed enclosed gearing.

**Inputs**
- Specific load factor: ks = 120 N/mm
- Pitch line velocity: v = 1.5 m/s
- Lubrication method: Splash / dip
- Operating temperature: T = 65 °C

**Outputs**
- Index factor: ks / v = 120 / 1.5 = 80.0
- Recommended viscosity grade: ISO VG 320 to ISO VG 460 (AGMA 6EP / 7EP)
- High viscosity required to prevent boundary metal-to-metal contact at low shearing velocities.
