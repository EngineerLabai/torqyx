## Example 1: Splash Lubricated Machine Gearbox

Selecting oil grade for a machine tool feed gearbox.

**Inputs**
- Pitch diameter: $d = 120\text{ mm}$
- Rotational speed: $n = 950\text{ rpm}$
- Pitch line velocity: $v = \pi \times 120 \times 950 / 60\,000 = 5.97\text{ m/s}$
- Tangential force: $F_t = 1800\text{ N}$
- Face width: $b = 30\text{ mm}$
- Specific tooth load: $k_s = 1800 / 30 = 60\text{ N/mm}$

**Outputs**
- Index factor: $k_s / v = 60 / 5.97 = 10.05$
- Recommended grade: ISO VG 220 (CLP 220)
- Optimal operating oil temperature range: 45°C to 65°C.

## Example 2: Forced-Feed Turbomachinery Gear

Selecting synthetic oil for high-pitch-line velocity pinion.

**Inputs**
- Pitch line velocity: $v = 35\text{ m/s}$
- Specific load: $k_s = 40\text{ N/mm}$
- Index factor: $40 / 35 = 1.14$
- Lubrication method: Pressurized circulating spray jet

**Outputs**
- Recommended grade: ISO VG 46 to ISO VG 68
- Low viscosity prevents severe fluid shearing losses and cavitation at high mesh entry speeds.
