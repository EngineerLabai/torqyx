<Callout type="info" title="Engineering Scope">
This tool maps tooth specific loading, pitch line velocity, and operating temperatures to recommended ISO 3448 / AGMA 9005 industrial gear oil viscosity grades (ISO VG 68 through VG 460) to maintain elastohydrodynamic lubricant film integrity.
</Callout>

## Governing Formulas & Methodology

Kinematic pitch velocity and unit loading:

<Formula label="Pitch Velocity and Tooth Load Factor">
v = (π · d · n) / 60000 | ks = Ft / b
</Formula>

Empirical lubrication ratio index:

<Formula label="Viscosity Selection Index">
Index = ks / v = (Ft / b) / v
</Formula>

## Thermal & Additive Requirements

<Callout type="warning" title="Viscosity Selection Criteria">
Operating at higher viscosity than required leads to excessive churning friction, increased gearbox bulk temperature, and hydraulic power loss. Conversely, under-viscous lubricants cause asperities to penetrate the oil film, resulting in accelerated adhesive wear and tooth surface pitting.
</Callout>
