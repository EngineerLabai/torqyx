<Callout type="info" title="Engineering Scope">
This tool calculates the orthogonal 3D gear mesh reaction forces—tangential (Ft), radial (Fr), and axial (Fa)—from transmitted shaft power, speed, pitch geometry, and tooth angles under ISO 6336 and AGMA 2001 principles.
</Callout>

## Governing Formulas & Methodology

Shaft torque derived from mechanical power:

<Formula label="Torque and Tangential Force">
T = 9550 · P / n | Ft = 2000 · T / d
</Formula>

Radial separating force and axial thrust resolution:

<Formula label="Radial and Axial Forces">
Fr = Ft · (tan(α_n) / cos(β)) | Fa = Ft · tan(β) | Fnet = √(Ft² + Fr² + Fa²)
</Formula>

## Application to Downstream Machine Elements

<Callout type="warning" title="Bearing Reaction Design">
Tangential force (Ft) generates shaft bending and transmission torque; radial force (Fr) creates additional radial bearing loads; axial thrust force (Fa) creates shaft tilting moments and requires thrust bearings (such as tapered roller or angular contact bearings).
</Callout>
