## Calculation Explanation

<Callout type="info" title="How to use">
<StepList title="Step-by-step mass estimation" steps={["Enter gear outer diameter (D in mm), inner bore diameter (d in mm), and face width (b in mm)", "Specify material density (ρ in kg/m³) and lightening factor (web pockets, lightening holes)", "Evaluate solid blank mass, optimized lightweight mass, and raw volume"]} />
</Callout>

<Formula label="Gear Blank Mass & Volume Estimation">
V = (π / 4) · (D² - d²) · b
V_m3 = V · 1e-9
m_solid = V_m3 · ρ
m_light = m_solid · (1 - Lightening Factor)
Δm = m_solid - m_light
</Formula>

**Assumptions**
- Primary blank modeled as an annular hollow cylinder of uniform density.
- Lightening factor accounts for web recess turning, lightening holes, or forged rim-and-spoke geometries.
- Material densities: Carbon/Alloy Steel (≈ 7850 kg/m³), Cast Iron (≈ 7200 kg/m³), Aluminum (≈ 2700 kg/m³), Bronze (≈ 8800 kg/m³).

**Units**
- Diameters (D, d), face width (b): mm
- Volume (V): m³ (or cm³)
- Density (ρ): kg/m³
- Mass (m_solid, m_light): kg

**Engineering Standards**
- ISO 21771 (Cylindrical involute gears — Geometry concepts)
- ISO 1940-1 (Mechanical vibration — Balance quality requirements for rotors in a constant state)
- DIN 3960 (Definitions and parameters for involute cylindrical gears)

<Callout type="warning" title="Structural Rigidity and Rim Thickness">
When lightening large gear blanks, maintain rim thickness under tooth root: s_R ≥ 1.5 · m (where m is gear module) to avoid root fatigue bending failures caused by web deflection under dynamic tooth contact.
</Callout>

## Examples

### Example 1: Webbed Industrial Steel Bull Gear

Evaluating weight reduction on a 250 mm reduction stage spur gear.

**Inputs**
- Outer diameter D = 250 mm
- Inner bore diameter d = 60 mm
- Face width b = 40 mm
- Material: Structural steel (ρ = 7850 kg/m³)
- Target web lightening factor: 0.20 (20% reduction via bilateral pocket machining)

**Outputs**
- Solid volume: V = (π / 4) × (250² - 60²) × 40 = 1.850e6 mm³ = 0.00185 m³
- Solid blank mass: m_solid = 0.00185 × 7850 = 14.53 kg
- Lightened operating mass: m_light = 14.53 × (1 - 0.20) = 11.62 kg
- Net mass saved: Δm = 2.91 kg per gear wheel.

### Example 2: Lightweight Aluminum Robot Actuator Gear

Comparing aluminum vs steel gear blank inertia.

**Inputs**
- Outer diameter D = 120 mm, Bore d = 30 mm, Face width b = 15 mm
- Material: 7075-T6 aluminum (ρ = 2810 kg/m³)
- Lightening factor: 0.15

**Outputs**
- Solid mass: m_solid = 0.447 kg
- Lightened mass: m_light = 0.380 kg
- Low rotational mass significantly reduces motor rotor inertia and increases servo bandwidth.
