## Example 1: Reducer Stage Backlash Verification

Estimating initial circumferential backlash for a heavy conveyor gearbox.

**Inputs**
- Module: $m = 6.0\text{ mm}$
- Face width: $b = 75\text{ mm}$
- Center distance: $a = 240\text{ mm}$
- Operating temperature rise: $\Delta T = 50^\circ\text{C}$

**Outputs**
- Nominal backlash: $j_{nom} = 0.04 \times 6.0 + 0.001 \times 75 = 0.315\text{ mm}$
- Acceptable backlash tolerance band: $0.252\text{ mm}$ to $0.378\text{ mm}$
- Thermal clearance adjustment: Accommodates housing expansion without risking thermal seizure.

## Example 2: Planetary Drive Sun-Planet Mesh

Sizing backlash for high-torque compact planetary gears.

**Inputs**
- Module: $m = 2.5\text{ mm}$
- Face width: $b = 30\text{ mm}$
- Center distance: $a = 75\text{ mm}$

**Outputs**
- Nominal backlash: $j_{nom} = 0.04 \times 2.5 + 0.001 \times 30 = 0.130\text{ mm}$
- Minimum clearance: $0.104\text{ mm}$
- Maximum clearance: $0.156\text{ mm}$
