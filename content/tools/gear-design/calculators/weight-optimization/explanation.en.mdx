<Callout type="info" title="Engineering Scope">
This tool calculates the volume, solid annular mass, and web-lightened weight of cylindrical gear blanks, supporting rapid inertia estimation and material optimization for power transmissions.
</Callout>

## Governing Formulas & Methodology

Annular hollow cylinder volume and solid mass:

<Formula label="Volume and Solid Mass">
V = (π / 4) · (D² - d²) · b | m_solid = V · 1e-9 · ρ
</Formula>

Optimized lightened mass accounting for pocket or web turning:

<Formula label="Lightened Mass and Weight Reduction">
m_light = m_solid · (1 - Lightening Factor) | Δm = m_solid · Lightening Factor
</Formula>

## Manufacturing & Dynamic Considerations

<Callout type="warning" title="Rotational Balance and Rim Rigidity">
Lightening holes and cutouts must be symmetrically spaced to maintain dynamic balance quality (ISO 1940-1 Class G2.5 or G6.3). Backing rim thickness below the root circle must never fall below 1.5 · module to prevent web flexure under load.
</Callout>
