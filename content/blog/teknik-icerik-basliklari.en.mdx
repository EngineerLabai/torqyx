## Why title selection matters

The right title helps your content reach the target audience. In engineering-focused blog posts, clear and specific titles work best.

## How to choose a title

- Define the main idea of the article.
- Identify key terms.
- Keep the title short and descriptive.
- Make sure it answers the reader's question.

## Sample title sets

- "Weekly torque calculation checklist"
- "What to consider when choosing content titles"
- "Clear topic titles for engineering blog posts"

## Categories and tags

Categories help group topics. Tags help filter articles by more detailed keywords.

## Conclusion

Every new post should connect to previous content and make it easy for readers to follow.

## Making engineering titles stronger

A technical title should show the problem the reader can solve. "Bearing life" is broad, while "How to calculate L10 bearing life from C and P values" is more useful because it names both the method and expected inputs. The same principle applies to pipe pressure loss, bolt preload, tolerance selection, and unit conversion articles.

After choosing the title, keep the description just as specific. A visitor should quickly understand whether the page is theory, a worked example, a standards summary, or a tool guide. Avoid vague marketing language; state the boundary, assumption, and reference used by the article. That makes the content easier to trust.

The final step is internal linking. Each technical article should point to at least one calculator, guide, or glossary term. These links help the reader continue the workflow and prevent the archive from feeling like a loose list of short notes. When title, description, and internal links work together, the blog becomes a practical engineering knowledge map.
